## DriverPass Project Reflection

### Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client for this project was DriverPass, a company that wanted to help students prepare for their DMV driving exams. They wanted a web-based system where customers could create accounts, select training packages, take online practice tests, and schedule driving lessons. The system also needed to help employees manage appointments, customer information, driver schedules, and reports. The overall goal was to make things easier for customers while giving the business a better way to manage its daily operations.

### What did you do particularly well?

I think I did particularly well at breaking down the requirements and figuring out what each user needed from the system. I made sure to consider the customers, secretary, drivers, owner, and IT officer when working on the design. I also think I did a good job using the different UML diagrams to show how the system would work. The activity diagrams helped explain the registration and scheduling processes, while the sequence and class diagrams showed how the different parts of the system connect.

### If you could choose one part of your work to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of my work, I would spend more time improving my UML diagrams. I would make sure they are easier to follow and that the information matches the written requirements. I would also review the scheduling process to make sure it clearly shows what happens when a driver or vehicle is unavailable. I think making these changes would help someone who is not familiar with the project understand the design better.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it important?

I interpreted the user's needs by reviewing the requirements and identifying what each person would need to do in the system. Customers needed to schedule lessons and access training materials, while employees needed to manage appointments, record lesson information, and maintain customer accounts. I used these needs to create the functional requirements and UML diagrams. I think considering the user's needs is one of the most important parts of software design because a system needs to solve the problems it was created for. Even if the technology works, it doesn't mean much if the people using it can't accomplish what they need to do.

### How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future?

My approach to designing software is to understand the problem before trying to come up with a solution. I would start by gathering requirements, identifying the different users, and figuring out what the system needs to accomplish. From there, I would break the project into smaller parts and use UML diagrams to organize everything. In the future, I would also want to get feedback earlier and review the design throughout the process instead of waiting until the end. I think taking the time to plan things out and catch problems early can make development easier and help create a better final product.
